#About Me
Hi! My name is Isabella Alzamora, and I am a mechanical engineering student concentrating in biomedical engineering. I am particularly interested in how mechanisms, systems, and materials can be understood and applied to solve practical problems, especially within medical technology.

I would describe myself as an analytical, persistent, and hands-on engineer who also values creativity in the design process. My experience includes mechanical design, CAD, fabrication, and biomedical research. I have significant experience using Creo and currently work as an engineering lab assistant in the Super Fabrication Lab, where I assist students and clients with machinery, fabrication, and CAD design.

My biomedical engineering experience has allowed me to apply these skills to healthcare-related challenges. As a biomedical engineering lab assistant, I designed a mold for microvessel formation and contributed to experimental iterations involving microvessels and cell formation. Through the Biomedical Engineering Society, I have also collaborated with 49er Robotics to integrate a pulse oximeter into a companion robot, with plans to continue developing the system.

These experiences have strengthened my interest in medical technology and in using mechanical engineering to improve healthcare. As I continue developing professionally, I hope to expand my technical skills and contribute to practical, well-designed solutions to complex biomedical problems.

Defending an Engineering Decision

To me, defending an engineering decision means being able to explain why a particular choice was made using calculations, evidence, requirements, and comparisons rather than personal preference alone. I currently feel confident in my ability to make and explain engineering decisions, while recognizing that there are still many methods and tools I have yet to learn. As I gain more experience, I want to become better at supporting my decisions with quantitative evidence and clearly communicating why one solution is more appropriate than another.
